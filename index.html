Deal Execution Roadmap

How Our Brokerage Process Works

A disciplined 4-phase transaction methodology engineered to protect client confidentiality and achieve optimal multiples.

01

Consult & Strategy

We evaluate your financial statements, calculate recasting add-backs, and determine an accurate market valuation range.

02

Match & Blind Profile

We market a confidential blind profile to our 12,500+ pre-vetted buyer network. Detailed CIMs require signed electronic NDAs.

03

Evaluate & Diligence

We vet proof of funds, negotiate competing Letters of Intent (LOIs), and coordinate CPA and legal due diligence rooms.

04

Close & Transition

We manage SBA/bank escrow funding, closing documentation, lease assignments, and a structured handover protocol.
