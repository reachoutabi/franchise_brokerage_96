Our 4-Phase Franchise Acquisition Roadmap

We guide you every step of the way—from personality and financial alignment through FDD Item 19 audit and Discovery Day negotiation.

01

Diagnostic & Profile

We evaluate your liquid net worth, risk profile, leadership style, and preferred industry verticals to create an ideal franchisee avatar.

02

Brand Matching & Territory

We present 3 to 5 curated franchise brands with open, protected territory in your target market and coordinate initial franchisor introductions.

03

FDD Review & Validation

We dissect Item 19 Financial Performance Representations, audit franchisee turnover, and facilitate confidential peer validation calls.

04

Discovery Day & Launch

We prepare you for executive Discovery Day interviews at corporate headquarters, finalize SBA funding, and coordinate lease approvals.

Strategic Comparison

Franchise Resale vs. Ground-Up Territory Launch

Depending on your capital strategy, you can either step into an operating unit with proven day-one cash flow, or develop an untouched demographic with maximum long-term upside.

Franchise Resales (Existing Cash Flow)

Acquire an open, operating store with trained staff, active customers, and verifiable P&L history. Immediate cash flow from day one with established vendor accounts.

New Territory Development (Pristine Demographics)

Secure the top real estate and demographic corridor in your city before competitors enter. Custom store design and complete multi-unit development schedules.
