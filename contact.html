Contact & Consultation FAQ

What to expect when reaching out to our certified business brokers.

Yes. All conversations with our managing brokers and valuation specialists are held in strict confidence under licensed fiduciary standards. No public record or disclosure is generated, and your personal contact information is never shared with third parties.
Our brokerage desk responds to all confidential inquiries within 4 business hours. If you submit our interactive valuation form, a managing partner can typically schedule a 45-minute virtual review within 24 to 48 hours.
Vanguard operates primarily on a performance-based success fee model (Lehman formula), meaning our advisory fee is collected at closing from transaction proceeds. We charge zero upfront marketing fees on qualified business mandates, aligning our incentives with achieving maximum valuation for you.
On average, our private business transactions close within 120 to 180 days from CIM launch to escrow release. Timeline varies based on industry sector, clean accounting records, price realism, and whether the buyer requires SBA 7(a) financing (typically 45-60 days for bank underwriting).
For businesses qualifying for SBA 7(a) acquisition financing, buyers typically need 10% to 20% of the total purchase price in verified liquid cash (savings, marketable securities, or 401(k) ROBS rollovers). In transactions involving seller financing or franchise storefronts, down payments commonly range from 30% to 50%.
