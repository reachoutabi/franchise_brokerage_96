Our Heritage

Why We Founded Vanguard: Eliminating Predatory Upfront Fees and Careless Leaks.

In 2002, our founding partners—former corporate M&A attorneys and seasoned business intermediaries—observed a deeply broken business brokerage industry. Traditional brokers routinely charged exorbitant upfront marketing retainers, listed businesses with unrealistic multiples, and leaked confidential operational data that damaged seller reputations with staff, vendors, and competitors.

Vanguard was established on a radical, fiduciary premise: 100% performance alignment. We charge zero upfront retainers on qualified mandates. We prepare certified, defensible Seller’s Discretionary Earnings (SDE) recasts, and we protect every client with a strict two-tier blind marketing protocol.

Over the last two decades, this commitment has driven over $420,000,000 in closed transaction volume across 850+ completed acquisitions and franchise syndications.

Our Operating Principles

The Four Vanguard Fiduciary Pillars

Every partner, valuation analyst, and deal coordinator operates strictly under our institutional code of representation.

Absolute Confidentiality

Your employees, competitors, and suppliers never discover that your business is on the market. All initial marketing is strictly blind. Full disclosures require executed NDAs and verified proof of liquid funds.

Valuation Precision

We don’t guess asking prices. We perform forensic recasting of tax returns and P&Ls to extract normalized Seller’s Discretionary Earnings (SDE), defending your enterprise multiple with institutional comparables.

Zero Upfront Retainers

Unlike fee-churning consultancies, our revenue is tied 100% to successfully closing your sale at or above valuation. If your transaction does not close, you owe us nothing for marketing, CIM preparation, or buyer vetting.

Pre-Vetted Liquidity

We prevent tire-kickers and unqualified browsers from wasting your time. Every prospective buyer undergoes banking verification, credit solvency reviews, and criminal background checks prior to scheduling calls.
