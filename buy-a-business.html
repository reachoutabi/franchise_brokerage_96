Acquisition Roadmap

The 5 Steps to Buying a Business

A step-by-step institutional framework guiding prospective acquirers from criteria definition to key handover.

1

Define Criteria

Clarify industry preferences, liquid capital down payment, and active vs. passive management roles.

2

Sign NDA & CIM

Execute our confidential agreement to unlock full tax returns, equipment lists, and lease terms.

3

Meeting & LOI

Attend confidential owner interviews and submit a non-binding Letter of Intent with purchase structure.

4

Due Diligence

Conduct 30-45 day CPA audits, inventory counts, lease assignment approvals, and bank underwriting.

5

Closing & Escrow

Finalize closing documents, wire balance into escrow, and begin seller training and transition period.
